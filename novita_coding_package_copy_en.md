## 4. Rules Modal Content

### 4.1 Modal Title
> **Novita Coding Plan Rules & Terms**

### 4.2 Detailed Rules

#### 📋 Plan Validity Period
- Plans are billed by **calendar month** and activate immediately upon purchase
- Unlimited API calls within validity period until quota is exhausted or plan expires
- Renewal reminders sent via email/notification 7 days before expiration

#### 💳 Billing Method
- Supports **monthly/annual** payment (annual saves 10%)
- Accepts credit card, PayPal, Alipay, and other payment methods
- Invoices sent to email within 24 hours of purchase

#### 🔄 Quota Usage Rules
- Quota is **valid for current month only**, does not roll over
- After quota exhaustion, automatically switches to pay-as-you-go (standard API pricing)
- Real-time quota tracking available in console

#### ⚙️ Model Usage Details
- Lite plan: Includes deepseek-v-3.2, gim-4.7
- Plus plan: All Lite models + minimax-m2.1, kimi-k2.5
- Max plan: All Plus models + full Premium suite (continuously updated)

#### 🔐 API Access Method
- No separate model setup or endpoint creation needed
- Use unified API Key to access all plan models after purchase
- Fully compatible with OpenAI API standard, supports mainstream IDE tools

#### 📞 Technical Support
- **Lite/Plus plans**: Email support (24-hour response)
- **Max plan**: Dedicated ticket system + Discord priority channel (12-hour response)

#### ❌ Refund Policy
- **Within 7 days** of purchase AND quota usage under 10%: Full refund available
- Beyond 7 days OR quota usage over 10%: No refunds
- Accounts banned for violations: No refunds

#### 🚫 Prohibited Activities

- Violations result in account ban without refund

#### 📢 Program Notes
- This is a **permanent product**, not a limited-time promotion

- Existing subscribers unaffected by price changes; renewal uses adjusted pricing

## 5. FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

### 5.1 FAQ Section Title
> **Frequently Asked Questions**

---

### 5.2 FAQ List

#### ❓ Q1: Can existing users purchase Coding Plans?
**A:** Yes! Both new and existing users can purchase, as long as they've never bought a Coding Plan before. Existing plan holders can also upgrade to higher tiers.

---

#### ❓ Q2: Do I need to separately enable models or create endpoints after purchasing?
**A:** No. Plan models work just like regular API access—you can call them directly after purchase. See our [API Documentation](https://novita.ai/docs) for details.

---

#### ❓ Q3: Will my account balance be charged after plan quota is exhausted?
**A:** Yes. To prevent service interruption, the system automatically switches to pay-as-you-go billing from your account balance after plan quota runs out. Monitor your usage via notifications and balance alerts, and renew or top up promptly.

---

#### ❓ Q4: Are there usage restrictions on plan models?
**A:** Plan models work exactly like regular API access, supporting all standard parameters and features. See our [API Documentation](https://novita.ai/docs) for full details.

---

#### ❓ Q5: How do I check my quota usage?
**A:** Log into [Novita Console](https://novita.ai/dashboard), navigate to the "Coding Plans" page to view real-time quota balance, usage details, and expiration date.

---

#### ❓ Q6: What payment methods are supported?
**A:** We accept credit cards (Visa/MasterCard/Amex), PayPal, Alipay, WeChat Pay, and more.

---

#### ❓ Q7: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
**A:** Yes. You can disable auto-renewal in the console anytime. Your current cycle remains active, and billing stops after expiration.

---

#### ❓ Q8: What does "3x lite tokens" mean for the Plus plan?
**A:** Plus includes 1.5B tokens, equivalent to the total quota of 3 Lite plans (50M tokens each), but at a better price point and higher value.

---

#### ❓ Q9: Can multiple people share one plan?
**A:** Yes. You can share your API Key with team members, but note that quota is shared. We recommend Max plans for team usage to ensure sufficient quota.

---

#### ❓ Q10: Will my plan auto-renew after expiration?
**A:** Auto-renewal is enabled by default. You can disable it in the console if not needed. Renewal reminders are sent 7 days before expiration via email and notification.

---

#### ❓ Q11: How do I contact technical support?
**A:**
- **Email Support**: support@novita.ai (24-hour response)
- **Discord Community**: [Join Novita Discord](https://discord.gg/novita)
- **Ticket System**: Console → Help Center → Submit Ticket (Max plan priority response)

---

#### ❓ Q12: Will plan models be updated over time?
**A:** Yes! We continuously add new SOTA models to plans. Existing subscribers can use new models for free without extra charges.

## 9. Copywriting Style Guide

### 9.1 Language Style
- **Professional yet friendly**: Use developer-familiar terms (tokens, API, SOTA), avoid over-marketing
- **Data-driven**: Highlight quantifiable metrics like "1/5 price", "80% savings", "1.5B tokens"
- **Concise & direct**: Keep sentences under 20 words, avoid lengthy descriptions

### 9.2 Visual Hierarchy
1. **Core selling points** (extra-large font): Price, quota
2. **Feature details** (medium font): Model list, use cases
3. **Supplementary info** (small font): Rules, technical support

### 9.3 Trust-Building Elements
- ✅ 7-day money-back guarantee
- ✅ 5,000+ developers using
- ✅ 24/7 technical support
- ✅ OpenAI-compatible API standard

## Appendix: Competitive Analysis Insights

### Learnings from Cerebras
- ✅ Use "value conversion" to highlight ROI (e.g., "$48/day worth of value")
- ✅ Quantify speed advantages (we can emphasize "response time")
- ✅ Transparent pricing, no hidden fees

### Learnings from Featherless
- ✅ Color-code plan tiers (blue → green → purple → gold)
- ✅ Badge-style labels enhance visual appeal
- ✅ Accordion-style FAQ reduces page complexity

### Learnings from Z.ai
- ✅ Educational marketing (we can add "How to Integrate" tutorials)
- ✅ Emphasize tool ecosystem (list supported IDEs)
- ✅ Low entry price to attract trials ($3/mo → ours is $20/mo)
